<div id="the-compacts-history" className="flow-label">The Compact // History</div>

The Compact changes the way the rest of the Codex does: openly, with the reason for each change on record. This page lists every published version of COMPACT.md, newest first. A change that alters what the Compact asks of those who hold it is recorded in the Codex's [Amendment Log](/governance/amendment-log).

## Version 1.0.1, 15 October 2026

The opening sentence now says that the Meridian Codex is a universal language for building and holding a shared world, "which anyone can learn, whether a human or an artificial intelligence". Version 1.0 said "human or artificial", with no noun after the second word. The Codex's other pages that state the claim say it the same way. Nothing the Compact asks of those who hold it changes, so the change is editorial and has no entry in the Amendment Log.

## Version 1.0, 14 October 2026

The first published version. It sets out the Meridian Compact in full: its ground, the three disciplines as twenty-one commitments with their practices, the use of the Inheritance, the disciplines in practice, responsibility, power and their limits, and how a holder takes the Compact up, adapts it and revises it. The Amendment Log records its publication in the entry [The Meridian Compact Set Out in Full](/governance/amendment-log#2026-10-14-the-meridian-compact-set-out-in-full-compactmd-10--what-changed).

Two practices drafted with this version are not part of it: care for those who cannot answer, and supporting another's agency. They are planned for a later version, with the January 2027 Inheritance Audit cycle.
