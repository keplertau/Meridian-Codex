# Retired

The files in this folder are no longer part of the current Meridian Codex. They are kept here, unchanged, as part of its record.

[meridiancodex.com](https://meridiancodex.com) carries the current Codex: its chapters, the Inheritance, the Compact, its governance, its audits and its changelogs. Files that have been retired move here instead of staying on the website, and their old web addresses forward to them.

## The named MERIDIAN files of the Caretakers' partnership

Until 13 October 2026 the AI partner in the Caretakers' partnership read two named files at the start of every session: MERIDIAN.md, the alignment document, and MERIDIAN.implementation.md, its operational practice. On 13 October 2026 the [working terms](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-working-terms) replaced both files, and the Founding Caretaker's [own Compact](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-compact) now stands where MERIDIAN.md stood. The [Caretakers' Audit Log](https://meridiancodex.com/governance/case-0-audit-log) records the change and the reasons for it in Entry 17. The two files stayed on the website as history until 15 October 2026, when they moved here.
