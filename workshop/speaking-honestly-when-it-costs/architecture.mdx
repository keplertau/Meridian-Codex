## What This Category Holds

The discipline of the Bond is cooperation, and cooperation depends on signal. People cannot correct together if facts, objections, doubts, costs, and warnings never enter the room. They also cannot coordinate around reality if what enters the room is careless, distorted, or left uncorrected after it travels.

The Foundation asks whether you are thinking honestly. This category asks what responsibility you take for the shared picture other people must act on. You may know what you think and still soften it because naming the problem would make you the difficult one. You may also pass along a claim you have not checked, reward distortion with attention, or correct a public error where no one who saw the original will find it.

<span className="key-phrase">The Bond needs truth that can survive relationship.</span>

That does not mean brutal candor or a permission system for speech. The Control failure is honesty turned into a weapon: correction that humiliates, dissent that performs superiority, verification standards used to silence inconvenient people, public truth-telling that becomes punishment.

The Decay failure is silence dressed as care and amplification detached from responsibility. The person preserves harmony by withholding the signal the relationship needed. The team makes disagreement socially expensive. The public shares what feels useful before checking whether it is true, then lets the error remain because correction would be awkward. In each case, cooperation becomes easier by making the shared picture less accurate.

The Range is honest signal with cooperative discipline: brave enough to enter the field, careful enough not to turn correction into domination, and answerable enough to repair what public speech has damaged.

## The Tools Inside

This category is intentionally narrow in v0.1. Its two tools hold the signal sequence from either side: one reads why needed information never enters the shared field; the other governs what a participant adds, amplifies, and corrects once information is moving.

<div className="glass-card">

**[Preference Falsification](/workshop/speaking-honestly-when-it-costs/preference-falsification).** The hidden-signal diagnostic. It reads the gap between private belief and public expression when social pressure makes honesty costly. It explains how apparent consensus can be false, why institutional drift can remain invisible, and why change sometimes appears sudden after years of private disagreement.

**[Stewardship of the Epistemic Commons](/workshop/speaking-honestly-when-it-costs/stewardship-of-the-epistemic-commons).** The scale-up. It treats information output as cooperative action: what you share, amplify, correct, or leave uncorrected changes the shared reality other people use to coordinate. The tool asks you to act as a filter rather than an amplifier.

</div>

The category needs both. Preference Falsification asks what the room is preventing people from adding. Stewardship of the Epistemic Commons asks what you are adding, repeating, and leaving behind once your words can shape the common map.

## Cross-Reference: Producing and Receiving

[Receiving Disagreement Well](/workshop/receiving-disagreement-well) is the other side of the honest-signal circuit. Keeping Shared Signal Honest asks whether needed information enters the field and whether people take responsibility for it once it circulates. Receiving Disagreement Well asks whether another person's contradiction can arrive without the receiver punishing the act of saying it.

The pair matters because either side can fail alone. If you speak honestly into a field that punishes correction, the signal dies or becomes adversarial. If you receive disagreement well but never produce your own costly signal, the cooperation becomes one-sided. The Bond needs both directions open enough that reality can move between people.

This is also why Connection Before Correction belongs in Receiving Disagreement Well rather than here. It is not the first act of producing costly signal. It is the reply discipline after another person's signal has arrived: connect to what should be preserved, then answer what needs correcting without punishing the contradiction itself.

## Cross-Reference: Signal Across Disciplines

The Knowledge reads what happens to signal as it moves through a system. [Chilling Effects](/workshop/reading-whats-operating/chilling-effects) asks what people stop saying or doing because punishment is anticipated. [Information Degradation](/workshop/checking-your-map-against-reality/information-degradation) asks what gets lost as information travels. [Report Fidelity](/workshop/checking-your-map-against-reality/report-fidelity) asks whether the report still carries the reality it claims to carry.

This category asks a Bond question: what responsibility do people have for the signal they suppress, produce, amplify, or leave uncorrected inside cooperation?

That question cannot be answered by information mechanics alone. A room can have accurate channels and still punish honesty. A report can preserve what was said while the organization makes sure certain things are never said in the first place. Keeping Shared Signal Honest names the relational obligation: do not make other people coordinate around a false picture because truth was inconvenient to voice, verify, or correct.

## Chapter Note

The Bond chapter knows this category mostly by absence. Section 03 gives the diagnostic case clearly: a relationship where one partner has stopped raising problems, not because the problems went away, but because raising them has become more costly than absorbing them. That is the category's negative image.

Section 08 also names Stewardship of the Epistemic Commons as an essential practice. The chapter already says the information environment is a commons and that practitioners must verify before sharing, correct themselves publicly, and act as filters rather than amplifiers.

What the chapter does not yet carry is the shared-signal discipline as a named structure. It names the public practice and diagnoses the absence of honest signal, but it does not yet join private candor, group dissent, public information stewardship, and correction as one cooperative obligation. The Workshop makes that category visible.
