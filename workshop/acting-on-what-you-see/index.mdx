*Reality*

## The Work

Seeing is not the end of Reality. If what you see never changes what you do, seeing has become spectatorship with better vocabulary.

This category holds the moment after a person, team, or institution has seen something real: a pattern, a constraint, a harm, an incentive, an opening, or a failure. The question is what that sight warrants: acting, holding the present course, or waiting for a reason that can be named. That rules out three evasions: forcing the world to match a preferred model, calling delay humility, and using analysis as shelter from responsibility. Whatever follows has to fit the situation as it has actually been seen.

<span className="key-phrase">Acting on What You See is the discipline of turning perception into action that remains answerable to evidence and to those affected.</span>

## The Tools

<div className="glass-card">

**[Wu Wei](/inheritance/acting-on-what-you-see/wu-wei).** The source-inherited practice of non-forcing action: intervening from contact with conditions rather than from domination, impatience, or self-display. Ziran is carried inside this profile as the naturalness or self-so ordering that wu wei answers to, not as a separate v0.1 tool.

**[Satyagraha](/inheritance/acting-on-what-you-see/satyagraha).** The source-inherited practice of truth-force: acting publicly from fidelity to truth through nonviolent discipline, willingness to bear cost, refusal to coerce the opponent into compliance, and honest scrutiny of the pressure and burdens the action actually creates.
