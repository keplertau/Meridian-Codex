## Why This Category Exists

The Reality discipline cannot stop at accurate perception. A person can read a system well, name the real constraint, identify the incentive, see the harm, and still fail because nothing follows from the seeing.

Earlier chapter language leaned too heavily toward handing action to Bond. Bond does own the discipline of sustaining cooperation through cost: how people remain in relation, repair rupture, calibrate trust, and keep shared action alive. But before that cooperative problem appears, there is a Reality question: how does one act on what has been read?

Acting on What You See names that prior discipline. Its object is not cooperation itself. Its object is the relation between perception and movement. The category asks whether action is governed by what reality has disclosed, or whether it is governed by impatience, ideology, self-protection, spectacle, or endless postponement.

<span className="key-phrase">Reality needs an action discipline because insight can fail by becoming inert.</span>

## The Range Vantage

The Control failure is unaccountable imposition. A person sees something, or thinks they do, and treats that sight as sufficient authority to command. Intervention overrides local knowledge, dismisses consent and objection, or persists after evidence shows that it is causing harm. The model is protected from the people and conditions that could correct it.

The Decay failure is inert seeing. The person keeps observing, refining, qualifying, waiting for perfect conditions, or deferring all movement to some later collective process. The sight remains true enough, but it never obtains practical force. Seeing becomes a substitute for action.

The Range form is intervention governed by evidence, proportionate to the problem, and answerable to those who bear its effects. A binding limit may be warranted. Whoever imposes it must justify their authority, show why a less restrictive action would not adequately address the harm, and keep the decision open to challenge and revision.

A limit on extraction from a shared watershed can protect the resource while constraining someone who opposes the allocation. That opposition must remain speakable and receive an answer. The decision has to account for whose needs it protects, who carries the loss, and how its effects will be reviewed. Effective enforcement alone does not establish legitimacy; disagreement alone does not establish Control.

## The Anti-Passivity Caveat

Restraint still has to leave room for sufficient action. A limit that exists only on paper may leave the harm untouched.

The term "non-forcing" can be misread as a refined excuse for inaction. That would collapse the category into Decay. Read the available choices, including the consequences of delay, and choose an action whose scope and cost can be justified. Keep observing after acting: a warranted intervention can outlive the conditions that warranted it.

Wu Wei and Satyagraha both require the anti-passivity caveat. Wu Wei is action in contact with conditions, removing excess force while choosing a sufficient move. Satyagraha is disciplined nonviolent action under cost, preserving its commitment against coercing the opponent. Those specific commitments belong to their profiles. The category also has to admit warranted interventions that neither profile claims to supply.

## Relationship To Bond

Bond begins where action has to be sustained across people. Acting on What You See can involve other people, but its first question is not "how do we cooperate?" Its first question is "what action is faithful to what has become visible?"

The boundary matters. If all action is handed to Bond, Reality becomes contemplative. If Reality absorbs all cooperative sustaining, Bond loses its own object. The cleaner architecture is sequential and overlapping:

- Reality reads the world.
- Acting on What You See asks what movement follows from the reading.
- Bond asks how people sustain truthful, trustworthy cooperation through the costs that movement creates.

Satyagraha sits near the boundary. Its truth-force belongs here; its constructive program, opponent relationship, and repair of political community cross into Bond. This profile should therefore carry explicit cross-reference obligations rather than pretending the practice belongs to only one discipline.

## Candidate Disposition

For this v0.1 draft, Wu Wei and Satyagraha are admitted as profiles. Ziran is not admitted as a separate profile. The reason is not that ziran is unimportant; it is that ziran functions as a source concept inside the wu wei mechanism. Treating it as separate at v0.1 would risk producing a thin duplicate: naturalness named apart from the non-forcing action that answers to it.

Future audits should revisit the boundary. If a later tool profile can show a distinct practice contribution from ziran rather than a background metaphysical term, it can be reconsidered.

## Publication Checks

Before publication, this category needs four checks:

- Does it make action load-bearing without collapsing into activism as temperament?
- Does it distinguish warranted constraint from unaccountable imposition, and restraint from inaction?
- Does it keep Bond's cooperative domain intact?
- Do the source-inherited profiles preserve their lineages rather than using them as decorative names for generic action advice?
