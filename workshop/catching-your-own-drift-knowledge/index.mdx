*Reality*

## The Work

Your understanding can fail from within. Not only because evidence is missing, reports degrade, or the system is hard to see into. It can also fail because you drift.

The model starts protecting itself. The frame becomes too complete. Complexity becomes an excuse not to judge. Uncertainty becomes a place to hide. A person who began by trying to see reality begins using interpretation to avoid it: either by making one interpretation sovereign over everything, or by refusing to let any interpretation become accountable enough to guide action.

This category names the Reality version of catching your own drift. It asks whether your account of what is happening still answers to the evidence.

<span className="key-phrase">The danger is not only misunderstanding what is happening. It is failing to notice when your own methods have stopped testing your explanations or stopped leading to judgments you can stand behind.</span>

## The Failure Modes

<div className="glass-card">

**[Ideology](/inheritance/catching-your-own-drift-knowledge/ideology).** The Control failure: an interpretive frame becomes self-protecting, totalizing, and sovereign over evidence. Reality is allowed to appear only in forms the frame can already explain.

**[Paralysis](/inheritance/catching-your-own-drift-knowledge/paralysis).** The Decay failure: complexity, uncertainty, information, and second-order awareness dissolve judgment until nothing can be concluded or done.
